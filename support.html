Frequently asked

How does Ledgerly connect to my bank?
We use Plaid, a trusted financial-data provider, to securely link your accounts. Your bank login credentials are handled by Plaid and are never seen or stored by Ledgerly.
Is my financial data safe?
Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is tightly restricted. We never sell your personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Can I disconnect an account or delete my data?
Yes. You can disconnect a linked account at any time, and you can request full deletion of your account and data by emailing us.
Does Ledgerly give financial advice?
No. Ledgerly is an informational tool. It doesn't provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice — please see our Terms of Service.
How much does it cost?
Ledgerly is free during the early-access beta. We'll give you plenty of notice before any pricing changes.
