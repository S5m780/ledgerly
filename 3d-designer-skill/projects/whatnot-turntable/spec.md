# Whatnot card-display turntable

tags: turntable, gear, worm-gear-motor, 12v, pwm, bearing, enclosure, display

## Goal
A slim motorised turntable for a Whatnot livestream auction. A rocker switch selects **spin** or
**hold** (stay still to show one card); a knob sets the speed between roughly **2 and 5 RPM** so it
works as a slow background carousel of cards.

## Requirements
- 12 V DC worm-gear motor hidden inside the base; motor output drives a gear on a vertical shaft that carries the plate
- Plate speed adjustable, about 2–5 RPM (one turn every 12–30 s)
- Rocker switch: spin / hold.  Rotary knob: speed.  Barrel jack for a 12 V wall adapter
- Slim: base as low as the motor allows, plate ≥ 200 mm so several cards or a small stand fit
- All custom parts 3D-printable on a 220 × 220 × 250 mm bed without supports (plate optionally split in two)
- Off-the-shelf parts only from common hobby suppliers; no soldering beyond wires to pads/tabs
- Plate removable without tools for shipping; optional card easel that sits on the plate

## Non-goals
- Battery power, wireless control, position sensing

## Print bed
print bed: 220 x 220 x 250 mm
