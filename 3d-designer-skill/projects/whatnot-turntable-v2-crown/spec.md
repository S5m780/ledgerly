# Whatnot card-display turntable v2 — crown drive

tags: turntable, gear, crown-gear, bevel, motor, n20, 12v, pwm, bearing, enclosure, display, lid, plate

## Goal
Same product as v1 (spin/hold rocker, 2–5 RPM knob, hidden 12 V drive) but as slim as the
mechanism allows: the motor lies flat and drives the vertical axis through a right-angle gear
stage instead of a worm gearbox. Printed on a Bambu Lab H2S, assembled with a hex key and a
screwdriver only.

## Requirements
- Motor horizontal; right-angle stage = printed spur pinion + printed crown (face) gear on the vertical axis
- Base height under 30 mm, overall under 40 mm with the plate
- Plate 2–5 RPM adjustable from a panel knob; rocker switch spin / hold
- Fixed axle: the plate and gear rotate on bearings, no printed face rubs under load
- Every part prints flat without supports on the H2S (350 x 320 x 325 mm); plate in one piece
- Assembly: no glue, no soldering except motor leads, one strap screw pair, six lid screws, three plate screws
- Interference view compiles empty

## Non-goals
- Battery, wireless, position sensing

## Print bed
print bed: 350 x 320 x 325 mm
