Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ledgerly ("Ledgerly," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use our website and application (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password.
  • Financial account information obtained through our bank-connection provider, Plaid Inc., when you choose to link a financial account. This may include account balances, transaction history, account and routing identifiers, account type, and account holder details.
  • Content you enter, such as manual transactions, budgets, goals, notes, and the money "programs" you or our AI create.
  • Usage and device data, such as log data, IP address, browser and device type, and how you interact with the Service.

We do not collect or store your online banking username or password. Those credentials are handled directly by Plaid.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service, including budgets, insights, and AI-generated money programs.
  • To categorize and display your transactions and financial activity.
  • To communicate with you about your account, updates, and support requests.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, security issues, and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We use your financial data to power features you request. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. How we share information, and with whom

We disclose information only as described below, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Service providers. We share information with vendors who process data on our behalf, including Plaid (financial account connectivity), hosting and infrastructure providers, and analytics providers. These parties are bound by contract to protect the data and use it only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Ledgerly, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers. If Ledgerly is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.
  • With your consent. We share information for any other purpose disclosed to you at the time and with your permission.

Method of disclosure. Data shared with service providers is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS) through secure application programming interfaces (APIs). We limit disclosures to the minimum data needed for each provider's function.

4. Plaid

We use Plaid Inc. ("Plaid") to connect your financial accounts. By linking an account, you also agree to Plaid's practices. Plaid's handling of your information is governed by the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. You can learn more at plaid.com/legal.

5. Data security

We take reasonable and appropriate measures designed to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS, and encryption of sensitive data at rest.
  • Use of tokenized access to financial data via Plaid, so that we never receive your banking login credentials.
  • Access controls that limit who can access data on a need-to-know basis.
  • Monitoring and logging to help detect and respond to security events.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You may access, update, or correct your account information within the Service.
  • You may disconnect a linked financial account at any time.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us.
  • Depending on your location, you may have additional rights (for example, to access, port, or object to processing of your data). To exercise these, contact us at the email below.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at support@my-ledgerly.com.
