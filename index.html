The magic ✦
🧩

AI builds you a custom money program — in seconds

Describe any goal in plain English: "help me pay off $4k in credit-card debt," "save $300/month for a trip," or "show me what $125/week invested could become." Ledgerly's AI instantly builds a tailored, interactive tracker with the right fields, calculations, and insights. No two people's finances look alike — your tools shouldn't either.

💸

Personal & business, side by side

Track every expense and income stream in one ledger. Flag entries as personal or business and tag deductibles so the freelance/side-hustle math stays clean all year.

◷

Budgets that actually hold

Set monthly limits per category and a savings goal. Watch progress in real time and get nudged before you blow past a line — not after.

✦

AI insights, not just charts

A clear read on where you stand: what's trending, what's off track, and the single next action that moves you forward. In plain language.

🏦

Connect your bank

Securely sync transactions so your ledger stays current without manual entry — or add things by hand in a tap. Your call.
